Temperature scales

Celsius • Fahrenheit • Kelvin

Celsius uses °C; Fahrenheit uses °F; Kelvin uses K. The SI unit of temperature is kelvin.
📏

Temperature scales

Converting Celsius to kelvin

Temperature scales

Temperature in kelvin = temperature in degree Celsius + 273.15. Example: 27 °C = 300.15 K.
🧮

Converting Celsius to kelvin

Writing temperature correctly

Units

Write a space between number and unit: 37 °C, 98.6 °F, 310.15 K. Do not use a degree sign with kelvin.
